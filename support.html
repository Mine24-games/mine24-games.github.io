Support

Oil Baron Idle · mine24

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email mine24.corp@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the game version (Settings, at the bottom).

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device every few seconds and backed up automatically. If you reinstalled the game, progress from the old installation cannot be restored yet — cloud saves are coming in a future update.

The ad button is grey

Ads need an internet connection and a moment to load. Check your connection and try again in a minute.

I didn’t get a reward for an ad

The reward is given only if the ad is watched to the end. If you watched it fully and got nothing, email us with the time it happened.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear icon (top right) → Language. 12 languages are available.

The game is slow or my phone gets warm

Settings → Graphics → Low turns off shadows and effects.

How do I turn off ad tracking?

Settings → Ad settings in the game, or iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I reset the game?

Settings → Reset progress (tap twice to confirm). This cannot be undone.
