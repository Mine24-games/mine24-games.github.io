Privacy Policy

Oil Baron Idle (Нафтовий Магнат) · Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Oil Baron Idle (the “Game”), developed by the independent studio mine24 (“we”, “us”), collects and how it is used. Contact: mine24.corp@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account to play.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the Game is played and to fix crashes. This data is not linked to your name or email.
  • Game Center (achievements and leaderboard) is provided by Apple.
  • We do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings (language, sound, graphics) and tutorial state are saved locally on your device.

iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game progress is stored in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage) so it can be restored on a new device. This data is held by Apple and is not accessible to us. Using Settings → Reset progress in the Game deletes this copy as well.

Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard score (the number of stars earned) are sent to Apple Game Center and may be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads — you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. To deliver, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect: your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interactions.

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are shown without the advertising identifier and may be less relevant. In regions where it is required (e.g. EEA, UK), a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform is shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad settings inside the Game.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The Game uses Firebase Analytics to see how the Game is played (for example: tutorial completed, building built, purchase made, ad reward received, screens opened) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and error reports. Firebase may collect: an app instance identifier, device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region (derived from IP), in-game events, and — only if you allow tracking — the advertising identifier (IDFA). Crash reports contain technical information about the error.

We use this data only in aggregate to improve the Game and fix bugs. It is not linked to your name or email, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Learn more: Firebase privacy.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, “offline income is full”). They are created on the device and no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the Game settings or in iPhone Settings.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases (gem packs, Starter Pack, No Ads) are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The Game only receives a confirmation of which item was purchased.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests concern data held by Google (ads, Firebase) or Apple (iCloud, Game Center) — you can manage them through the in-game ad settings, your device’s tracking settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) and Google’s tools. For any question, email mine24.corp@gmail.com.

9. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device (and in your iCloud, if enabled) until you delete the app or reset progress. Advertising and analytics data is retained by Google according to its policies; analytics data — up to 14 months.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game changes (for example, when we add new features). The date above shows the latest version.
