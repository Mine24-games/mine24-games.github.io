Privacy Policy

Oil Baron Idle (Нафтовий Магнат) · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Oil Baron Idle (the “Game”), developed by the independent studio mine24 (“we”, “us”), collects and how it is used. Contact: mine24.corp@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account to play.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings (language, sound, graphics) and tutorial state are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads — you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. To deliver, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect: your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interactions.

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are shown without the advertising identifier and may be less relevant. In regions where it is required (e.g. EEA, UK), a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform is shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad settings inside the Game.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, “offline income is full”). They are created on the device and no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the Game settings or in iPhone Settings.

5. Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests concern Google’s advertising data — you can manage them through the in-game ad settings, your device’s tracking settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) and Google’s tools. For any question, email mine24.corp@gmail.com.

8. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app or reset progress. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game changes (for example, if we add cloud saves or analytics). The date above shows the latest version.
